The problem

Africa doesn't lack ambition.
It lacks access to precision.

01 — Capital

The machine costs more than the business

An industrial 5-axis CNC centre lands at USD 50,000–200,000+. For an independent workshop, a technical school or a spare-parts manufacturer, that is simply out of reach.

02 — Service

When it breaks, it stays broken

Closed controllers, no local technicians, parts on six-week import lead times. Imported machines routinely sit idle — we have commissioned industrial machines and watched it happen.

03 — Skills & Language

CAD & foreign UI are the real gatekeepers

Even with a machine, you need a CAD designer working in foreign software. A skilled machinist who knows metal — not SolidWorks — cannot produce a single part. The software barrier, not the steel, is the bottleneck.

A broken pump impeller, a mould insert or a mining spare is re-imported instead of remade — and industrial value leaves the continent every time.

Safety by design

An AI that can't hurt the machine — or you.

Handing a CNC to an AI sounds reckless. So we engineered it the other way round: the AI never has the last word. Five independent layers stand between a sentence and a spinning cutter.

Operator
Confirmation
Validation

MACHINE
1
Emergency stop never goes through the AIA deterministic rule outside the model triggers an immediate halt — tested in French and English, with local-language rules (Bambara, Swahili, Amharic) under native-speaker review — even if the AI is busy or offline.
2
Nothing moves without your tapEvery acting command displays what was understood, in which language, and why confirmation is requested. Ambiguous or unreviewed matches always require explicit approval.
3
Look-ahead trajectory validationThe full toolpath is simulated against the machine's physical limits before any motion. Any move that would collide or exceed travel is refused, not run.
4
Hard & soft limits, fail-safe wiringNormally-closed limit switches, active soft and hard limits, a streaming watchdog and ForceGuard cutting-load protection.
5
Questions are never orders“Why does the spindle…?” or “don't start it” strip action tools from the AI for that turn. A question or negation can never start a machine.

Never invents telemetry

Positions, alarms and limits come straight from the controller and its config — or the answer is “not available”. Zero hallucinated numbers, ever.

Safety vocabulary is reviewed

A local language only receives “trusted” status once a named native speaker has reviewed its safety words. Until then, the app asks before acting.

Auditable releases

Every AI turn is journaled with the exact model and vocabulary version in use — and release changes require proof against our automated test suite.
